# SG Science Explorer

SG Science Explorer is a front-end science learning prototype for Singapore primary school students.

## What It Does

The app helps students revise P3 to P6 science topics through topic cards, short explanations, quizzes, feedback, and a simple chatbot-style helper.

## Features

* P3 to P6 level selection
* Science topics such as Magnets, Digestive System, Matter, Electricity, Forces, and Food Webs
* Short revision notes with key terms
* Multiple-choice quiz questions
* Instant correct/wrong feedback
* Singapore-related science examples
* Mobile-friendly design
* Rule-based chatbot helper for common science questions

## How AI Helped

I used ChatGPT to help generate and improve the code. I provided the idea, tested the app, checked whether the features worked, and refined the design and content.
